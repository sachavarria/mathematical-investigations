A difference of one

Two places on a 4,096-tick clock. At each place, twelve marks are enough to recognize a boundary. The two inscriptions differ by a single mark. One begins at tick 1 and the other at tick 1,636.

Each pale cross-section follows a necklace block in the intervening history. Each pinprick records one emitted bit. A change of viewpoint gives their separation depth. The clock was enumerated when the piece opened. The duration of the movement belongs to the composition.
