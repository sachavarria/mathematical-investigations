# Local observation and temporal rank

Grandmama concatenates the primitive roots of the least-rotation representatives of length-n binary necklaces in colex order (compare representatives from the right). The origin is the first emitted symbol. Each state is the length-n forward window. Source: https://www.socs.uoguelph.ca/~sawada/papers/k_grandmama.pdf

Run python3 windows.py. Columns are order, number of states whose least rotation equals the representative of the block containing their starting bit, and total states. This is an exhaustive exponential-time diagnostic. A rank algorithm could combine block identification, offset inside the block, and the sum of primitive periods of earlier representatives. The least-rotation shortcut fails. The data establish neither algorithmic hardness nor an asymptotic rate.

Comparison: Granddaddy has a polynomial-time bounded-space decoder (https://debruijnsequence.org/db/rank). In a cyclic group generated by g, ranking the orbit of 1 under multiplication by g is exactly discrete logarithm. Its difficulty depends on representation, group family and computational model. Neither comparison transfers a complexity bound to Grandmama.
