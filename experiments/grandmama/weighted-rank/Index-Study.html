Reading the instrument

Grandmama visits every binary window once. A word that begins a full-length block gives us a recognizable boundary. Its tick is the total length emitted by the blocks before it.

Read across the two rails. A candidate word can emit several ticks or none. The blue surface connects the ends of two stored intervals. Its interior is interpolated. The copper lines follow the increments found during this query. Inspect the scan to spread those increments across the field.

The slider sets how many bits select a stored count. Each positive-depth build tests every candidate once. A second query at the same order and depth reuses that index. The ledger counts candidate tests, table reads and array bytes. The graphic uses the query trace and samples it for display at high orders.
