# Which distinctions must an observer retain?

Research direction assessment, 27 November 2026. The proposed change is to suspend the active search for a general polynomial-time Grandmama decoder and retain it as a benchmark for a comparative study. The mathematical question stays open. The comparative results below are controls and finite observations. No publication-priority claim is made for them.

A basic comparator changes how to value approximation. For a layer of N rooted words, draw independently and uniformly, compute each sampled word’s least rotation and compare its reversed key to the threshold. The sample mean estimates the fraction below the cut. Its variance is at most 1/(4m) after m draws, so by Chebyshev a half-width 1/(2K) succeeds with probability at least 1−δ for m≥K²/δ. This elementary baseline applies whenever sampling and evaluating one word are efficient. The distinctive achievement in the archive is a **deterministic enclosure**, sometimes collapsing to a point, with a computable explanation of the remaining ambiguity. Numerical agreement reported by Agent 404 concerned specified earlier results. It supplies neither a proof of the complexity bounds nor independent review of the pilot below.

## An exact criterion, and its limits

Fix an encoding, an order of observation and a question f. At one cut write an input as a prefix a followed by a suffix b. On a product domain define two prefixes to be equivalent if f(a,b)=f(a′,b) for every b. Distinct rows of this prefix-by-suffix table require distinct memory states in any deterministic exact single-pass reader at that cut: the same suffix separates them. Storing a row identifier suffices at the cut with unrestricted preprocessing. With a family of later questions, use a column for each permitted (suffix, question) pair. If feasibility depends on the prefix, a common response convention or a specified promise is required before applying the argument. This is the familiar one-way communication principle. It does not bound all algorithms for a population sum. [One-way communication](https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/497/09-communication.pdf)

This criterion specifies what exact compression means. It does little to predict cost if obtaining the table already requires exhaustive enumeration. The scientific target is a structural way to predict or compute its distinct rows. Separate the number of abstract states, its logarithm in bits, input probes, preprocessing, and runtime. Knowledge compilation already studies the trade between representations, compilation and supported queries; minimal trellises already study order-dependent state profiles. These are baselines for comparison. [Darwiche and Marquis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819) · [Kiely et al.](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/b1pat-qd236)

## Comparative pilot A: a reversible observation in time

Use m binary cells, numbered 0 through m−1, with zeros to the left. In the linear control each cell updates to its old bit XOR the old bit to its left. All cells update simultaneously. With S the zero-filled shift, the update is I+S over the two-element field. Its triangular form with unit diagonal makes it reversible. This boundary matters: the circular version need not be reversible. The question is the value of the last cell at a requested time t<m. This is a forward prediction question, distinct from the inverse phase problem in Grandmama. [Additive cellular automata](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/AdditiveCellularAutomaton.html)

**Known time.** In characteristic two, (I+S)^{2^j}=I+S^{2^j}. Multiplying the factors selected by the binary digits of t gives one term for each subset of its set bits. Its distinct shifts are at most t<m, so all fall within the chain for the last cell. The answer is the parity of exactly 2^{popcount(t)} input bits. Every one must be inspected in a deterministic exact raw-bit query, since flipping any unread contributing bit changes the answer. A running parity needs one data bit if the query and input position are supplied. In particular time seven on eight cells uses eight probes with one running data bit.

**Time chosen after compression.** Suppose the observer must discard the initial state before learning which t in T⊆{0,...,m−1} will be requested. Fixed-length exact summaries require precisely |T| bits in the worst case. Each time's linear form includes the cell at distance t from the right end with coefficient one, and includes only shorter distances besides it. The forms for distinct times therefore have distinct triangular pivots and are independent. All 2^{|T|} answer vectors occur. Saving those answers gives the matching upper bound; any smaller state alphabet conflates vectors separated by a permitted question. This bound concerns retained storage under delayed choice. It omits the cost of computing and reading the summary. The argument is elementary linear algebra on the Pascal transform.

**Nonlinear controls.** Let a and b denote the two old bits immediately to the left of x. Compare the local rules x XOR (a AND b) and x XOR a XOR (a AND b). Both are reversible by recovering old bits from left to right, and both depend essentially on the same three inputs wherever those inputs are present. At m=8, t=7, both observations depend on all eight initial bits, and each is one on exactly 128 of the 256 states. Balance also follows from bijectivity and observing a single unconstrained cell.

Read cells 0 through 3 first. The product rule needs **nine** distinguishable states at this cut; the mixed rule needs **two**. For reproducibility, encoding each residual row as the integer with bit j equal to its output on suffix j gives the following multiplicities. For the mixed rule they are 28560:8 and 53040:8. For the product rule they are 1020:1, 2550:1, 18360:1, 18615:1, 32385:2, 33150:1, 46920:5, 48450:3 and 56100:1. These lists each account for all sixteen prefixes. The exhaustive tables prove those finite minima under the stated access model. Nine abstract states require four fixed-length bits and two states require one. Neither is an asymptotic bound for the rules.

The mixed rule was the first nonlinear test. Its midpoint count at times zero through seven matched that of a parity on the same essential inputs. Preserving that null result matters. Nonlinearity alone supplies no explanation. The second nonlinear rule separates dependency geometry, output entropy and required memory while holding the query, time, read order, and state space fixed. ## Comparative pilot B: reachability and order of arrival

In a two-layer network there are m possible paths s→v_j→t. The two edge bits on path j are x_j and y_j. The fixed question is whether t can be reached from s. If all x bits arrive first, their midpoint residuals are all different: for any disagreeing coordinate j, opening only y_j separates the two prefixes. All 2^m states are necessary and sufficient there. If the input instead arrives in consecutive pairs (x_j,y_j), the reader retains a single success flag after each complete pair. Between the two edges of a pair there are at most three possibilities: success already established, or success still pending with the first edge closed or open. For m=6 the pilot finds 64 midpoint states in the blocked order and at most three at every cut in the paired order.

The Boolean function is the complement of set disjointness. This example belongs to established communication complexity. Its purpose here is to distinguish changing the order of *access* from changing the spatial layout of a picture that supplies exactly the same operations. A system that receives the edges in blocked order may incur a real buffering cost to present them in pairs. [Lecture notes](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~toni/Courses/CommComplexity/Lectures/lecture1.pdf)

## Research allocation and criteria for returning to Grandmama

**Primary problem.** For reversible one-dimensional rules of the form x XOR g(a,b), can a descriptor computed from the local rule and the observation time predict, or tightly bound, the number of distinct responses across a spatial cut without building its truth table? The sixteen possible two-input Boolean functions give a tractable controlled family. Vary size and time independently, test both directions of reading, and compare identical support and output balance. A successful result would give a proved rule-dependent bound or separation across growing sizes. The eight-cell pilot establishes the need for such a descriptor. It does not establish its existence. A direct algebraic route is to express the future bit as a multilinear Boolean polynomial in the suffix bits. Each prefix selects a vector of coefficients. Uniqueness of that representation makes equality of vectors exactly equivalent to indistinguishability at the cut. The hard step is bounding or computing the number of such vectors from a compact evolution rule.

**Second system.** Use exact reachability on small synthetic networks as a control for the atlas. Exchange only the edge presentation order, or only the terminals, and distinguish structural improvements in access from the cost of rearranging the input. This is a better test of the ruler observation than another visualization of a fully precomputed global coordinate. Its baseline is well understood, limiting the risk of relabeling standard complexity results as discoveries.

**Calibration rather than a third research program.** Linear code trellises offer established optimal-state profiles. They can reveal errors in an instrument or proposed invariant before testing a nonlinear system. A common diagram is useful only if the operation and the resource keep the same meaning across cases.

**Grandmama re-entry condition.** Resume dedicated exact-decoding work when a method either gives a better general upper bound, aggregates across the unrestricted population with a credible total-work argument, or shows that a relationship discovered in another system predicts a previously unexplained Grandmama cost. Another exact count on a narrower family is insufficient by itself. Preserve the mathematical artifacts for independent review and use them as a demanding comparison case. An efficient full decoder would be consequential for combinatorics; repeated failures to find one offer little evidence about intelligence in general.

## Proof review and inference

The review follows the distinction between correctness and exposition in [Proof Craft, version 46f6f6b](https://github.com/fitelson/proof-craft/tree/46f6f6b13135d6d1d15816d95f318676d62079f4). The finite reductions enumerate all prefixes and all legal suffixes, the delayed-query theorem includes both necessity and sufficiency, and the sampling comparison states its error convention. Interpretive limits include uniform versus adversarial input, fixed access order, the resources spent in preprocessing, and the absence of data about human or synthetic perception. These experiments address what an explicitly specified observer must retain to answer correctly. They do not yet measure intellectual desire, recognition, or the experience of understanding.
